## FAQ
1. What USDA zones do the plants grow in?  
Zone 9 and above.

2. Is there free shipping?  
No. Actual USPS shipping cost is added based on destination.

3. Are the plants perennials?  
Most plants are perennials in zone 9 and above, depending on microclimate.

4. Do I need to fertilize plants often?  
For better plant health, fertilize once a month during growing season.

5. Do I need to water plants often?  
Keep soil moist and water when the top inch feels dry.
