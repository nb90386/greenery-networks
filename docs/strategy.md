## Brand Direction

### Brand Positioning
Greenery Networks should feel like a premium curated garden boutique, not a faceless store. The brand is a specialist grower that hand-selects colorful live plants and ships them fresh to gardeners across the USA.

### Voice and Tone
- Warm, confident, and knowledgeable
- Professional but friendly
- Earthy and floral, never cheesy or salesy
- Clear and helpful, especially for plant care and shipping
- Never fake, never pushy, never gimmicky

### Emotional Goal
Visitors should feel:
- Trust in plant quality and safe shipping
- Excitement about receiving live colorful flowers
- Confidence that buying is simple and transparent
- Calm, not overwhelmed
- Inspired to grow a more colorful garden

### Trust Goals
The site must communicate:
- Real plants, real photos, real inventory
- Clear pricing: $12 per plant
- Clear shipping rules: USPS, USA only, actual cost added
- No hidden discounts or fake urgency
- Professional nursery brand, not a dropshipper
- Safe checkout and clear policies

## Logo Direction

### Recommended Direction
A clean blooming network mark or refined leaf/petal wordmark. This feels modern, premium, and scalable across web, packaging, and future socials.

### Concepts Considered
1. **Blooming Network Mark** — stylized flower with soft petals and subtle interconnected lines suggesting a network of gardeners and plants
2. **Leaf + Petal Wordmark** — refined serif or semi-serif wordmark with a subtle leaf or petal integrated into a letterform
3. **Circular Garden Emblem** — circular badge with a central flowering plant and brand name wrapped around

### Color Palette
- Primary: soft leaf green
- Secondary: warm floral pink
- Accent: light sky blue
- Neutral: white and warm off-white
- Text: deep earthy charcoal, not pure black

### Typography Direction
- Headings: elegant serif or semi-serif with warmth
- Body: clean modern sans-serif
- Avoid playful, childish, or default fonts

### Icon/Symbol Ideas
- Single stylized flower
- Leaf with subtle network nodes
- 4-inch pot silhouette combined with bloom
- Minimal line-art botanical icons for categories and care tips

## Visual Design System

### Color Tokens
- `--color-leaf`: soft green
- `--color-leaf-light`: light green
- `--color-pink`: soft floral pink
- `--color-pink-light`: blush
- `--color-sky`: light blue
- `--color-sky-light`: pale sky
- `--color-cream`: warm off-white background
- `--color-charcoal`: deep earthy text
- `--color-muted`: muted secondary text

### Typography
- Headings: elegant serif or semi-serif, warm and premium
- Body: clean sans-serif, highly readable
- Hero: large, tight tracking
- Section headings: medium-large, strong hierarchy
- Body: comfortable reading size, good line height

### Spacing
- Section padding: `py-20 lg:py-32`
- Container: `max-w-7xl mx-auto px-4 sm:px-6 lg:px-8`
- Generous whitespace between sections
- Card padding: `p-6` to `p-8`

### Buttons
- Primary: solid leaf green or floral pink, white text, rounded, clear hover state
- Secondary: outline with brand color
- No weak gray buttons
- Always tappable on mobile

### Cards
- Soft rounded corners
- Subtle shadow or border
- Light background
- Hover lift effect
- Clean internal spacing

### Product Cards
- Top: large product image
- Middle: plant name, short description
- Bottom: price + Add to Cart
- Hover: slight lift, image zoom or swap

### Navigation
- Sticky top
- Transparent or light background
- Logo left, links center/right, CTA right
- Mobile hamburger with smooth open menu

### Footer
- Light or slightly darker than background
- Organized columns
- Brand, links, policies, contact
- Clean, not cluttered

### Image Treatment
- Rounded corners on product photos
- Consistent aspect ratios
- Soft natural shadows if needed
- No harsh filters

### Backgrounds and Gradients
- Mostly white and cream
- Very subtle soft gradients using light green/pink/sky
- Occasional floral background texture, kept minimal
- No loud or random gradients

## Animation Plan

### Principles
- Premium subtle animation only
- Use Framer Motion only where it improves feel
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`
- Duration: 0.4 to 0.8 seconds
- Easing: easeOut or spring with low bounce
- Stagger: 0.06 to 0.12 seconds

### Hero Animation
- Headline and subheadline fade/slide in
- CTA buttons fade in with slight delay
- Hero image gently scales or drifts
- Optional floating leaf/petal accents, very subtle

### Section Reveals
- Fade-in and slight vertical slide on scroll
- Use intersection observer
- Keep duration 0.4–0.7s

### Product Card Hover
- Subtle lift
- Soft shadow increase
- Image zoom or secondary image reveal if available

### Navigation Animation
- Smooth background change on scroll
- Mobile menu slide/fade

### FAQ Animation
- Accordion open/close with smooth height transition
- Icon rotation on expand

### Page Transitions
- Light fade or slide between pages
- Avoid heavy transitions that slow navigation

### Animations to Avoid
- Bouncing
- Oversized parallax
- Random motion
- Slow or laggy effects
- Anything that distracts from content or conversion
