# Greenery Networks UI/UX Max Design System

## Creative North Star
Greenery Networks should feel like an Apple-level garden boutique:
- premium
- warm
- earthy
- vivid
- calm
- trustworthy
- botanical
- high-converting
- not generic AI-looking

The site should feel like a modern editorial plant shop, not a basic nursery template.

## Final Visual Direction
Blend:
1. Apple-style whitespace and polish
2. Luxury botanical editorial photography
3. Soft earthy ecommerce warmth
4. Smooth high-end motion
5. Clear Shopify-style buying flow

Keywords:
Botanical boutique, fresh blooms, curated garden, soft sunlight, spring color, clean commerce, premium plant care.

## Design Quality Bar
The site must look like a real brand customers would trust with live plant shipping.

Reject designs that look:
- flat
- empty
- template-like
- SaaS-generic
- AI-generated
- childish
- overly pastel
- cluttered
- low-effort
- stock-photo dependent

Every section needs:
- strong composition
- clear visual hierarchy
- beautiful spacing
- intentional image placement
- premium motion
- clear CTA
- mobile-first quality

## Color System
Use this as the refined palette:

- Garden White: #FFFDF8
- Soft Sage: #DDEEDC
- Fresh Leaf: #7FAE7B
- Deep Moss: #2F5D3A
- Sky Mist: #DDEFF7
- Petal Pink: #F6C7D8
- Blush Bloom: #FBE8EF
- Warm Soil: #7A5C45
- Charcoal Leaf: #1E2A22

Use white/cream as the base.
Use green for trust and nature.
Use pink as floral accent.
Use blue only as a soft freshness accent.
Use dark moss/charcoal for premium contrast.

Avoid neon green, harsh blue, purple SaaS gradients, and random rainbow colors.

## Typography Direction
Use a premium pairing:
- Elegant display serif for emotional headings
- Clean modern sans-serif for body and commerce UI

Suggested feel:
- Headings: editorial, soft, refined
- Body: clean, readable, trustworthy
- Product text: simple and clear

Avoid:
- boring default font look
- too many font weights
- cramped line-height
- tiny gray text

## Layout Direction
The homepage should feel cinematic and intentional.

Use:
- large hero
- layered image compositions
- oversized flower photography
- floating product cards
- soft background gradients
- editorial split sections
- product grid with strong image focus
- generous whitespace
- clear buying path

Avoid:
- simple centered text + button only
- repeated same card layouts
- generic 3-column feature blocks everywhere

## Hero Section Requirements
The hero must be the strongest section.

It should include:
- emotional headline
- beautiful real plant imagery
- layered image/card composition
- soft floral background gradients
- animated entrance
- CTA: Browse Flowering Plants
- secondary CTA: Learn Plant Care
- trust note: Live starter plants · 4-inch pots · USA shipping
- shipping clarity: USPS shipping calculated based on destination

The hero should feel like:
“You just stepped into a curated online garden.”

## Product Card Requirements
Product cards must feel ecommerce-ready.

Each card should include:
- real plant image
- product name
- price: $12
- badge: 4-inch starter pot
- badge: Zone 9+
- stock: 5 available, shown subtly
- CTA: View Plant or Add to Cart later
- hover image zoom
- card lift
- premium shadow/border
- soft rounded corners

## Image Treatment
Use real plant images heavily.

Image style:
- large
- bright
- vivid
- softly rounded
- natural crops
- editorial framing
- subtle shadows
- hover zoom
- layered hero collage

Do not make images tiny.
Do not bury the product photos.
Do not use fake stock imagery if real images exist.

## Animation System
Use motion aggressively but tastefully.

Required:
- hero stagger reveal
- floating hero cards
- soft parallax-style movement
- product card stagger
- scroll reveal by section
- image hover zoom
- CTA hover glow/lift
- FAQ open/close animation
- mobile menu animation
- smooth page feel

Animation rules:
- premium, smooth, not goofy
- fast enough to feel responsive
- avoid bouncy childish effects
- avoid laggy heavy effects
- respect mobile performance

## Conversion System
Every page should move visitors toward buying.

Important conversion elements:
- clear CTA above fold
- “Browse Flowering Plants” CTA repeated
- shipping clarity before purchase
- price clarity
- plant care trust
- FAQ near buying sections
- no confusing copy
- no fake reviews
- no personal names

## Page-Specific Direction

### Home
Must feel most premium.
Sections:
1. Premium nav
2. Cinematic hero
3. Featured flowering plants
4. Why gardeners choose Greenery Networks
5. Shipping clarity
6. Plant care preview
7. Brand story
8. FAQ preview
9. Final CTA
10. Footer

### Shop
Must feel like a real product collection.
Include:
- collection hero
- product grid
- filter/category chips if useful
- shipping note
- care badges
- clear product cards

### About
No personal names.
Focus on:
- love of gardening
- spreading colorful flowers
- curated flowering plants
- professional online nursery feel

### Plant Care
Make this beautiful and useful.
Include:
- Zone 9+
- watering
- fertilizing
- microclimate
- shipping arrival care

### FAQ
Make it polished, animated, and easy to scan.

### Contact
Email-only professional contact.
No fake address.
No social links unless created later.

## 21st.dev Magic Usage
Use Magic only as a premium component accelerator.

Best Magic targets:
- hero section
- product cards
- animated feature section
- FAQ accordion
- CTA section
- gallery section

Do not paste random Magic components that clash.
All Magic output must be restyled to match Greenery Networks.

## Final UI Checklist
Before saying done, verify:
- homepage looks premium at first glance
- real images are used
- mobile layout works
- CTAs are obvious
- product cards look ecommerce-ready
- animations are smooth
- brand colors are consistent
- no fake reviews
- no personal names
- shipping info is clear
- build passes
